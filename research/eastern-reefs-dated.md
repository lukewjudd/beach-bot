# Eastern-suburbs reefs: dated surf sessions for backtesting

Compiled 9 Nov 2026. Day precision only. Every date below is stated in the source (video title, caption, article) or
deduced from an upload timestamp plus "this morning / today" wording (marked *ts*). Undated sources are listed
separately under "Undated / weak" and should not be used in the backtest.

## Sources and how they were used

- **"Surf Check" YouTube channel** (`UCFwJJLi6Wqo1b7PX33zoobA`). A Sydney local posts a daily filmed surf check with
  the date and time in the title and a short written verdict (swell, wind, tide, which banks are working). 480 uploads,
  mostly 2023-05 to 2026-10, mostly Bronte/Tamarama, also Bondi, Maroubra, Malabar, Wedding Cake Island and "Pebble"
  (= the Coogee/Clovelly bombie). This is the backbone of the dataset: it includes many **flat and not-working days**,
  which are hard to find elsewhere. All listed with links. In his wording "the point" at Bronte = the Bronte reef.
- Other YouTube uploads with dated titles or descriptions (Glenn Duffus/"Southie" videos, Andrew Explores, hotndelicious,
  mySURF tv, Surfvids, 9News/7NEWS).
- Tracks Magazine articles (fetchable).
- WannaSurf and surf-forecast.com spot guides for conditions.
- **Blocked (403 / bot check), skipped:** swellnet.com, aquabumps.com, coogeenews.com.au, surfer.com, theinertia.com,
  kidsnews.com.au, pacificlongboarder.com. Only their search-engine snippets were used, and only where they carry a date.

Abbreviations: verdict **GOOD** (working / pumping / epic), **OK** (surfable, mixed), **POOR** (swell present but spot
not working: wrong direction, blown out, too small for the spot), **FLAT**.

## Conditions each spot needs (as stated by sources)

**Clovelly / Coogee Bombie (Pebble)**
- Both directions; "sucky right with a major left when big"; the left breaks into the cliff. Swell E-NE; starts working
  around 1.5-2 m (5-6 ft) and holds 5 m+; best wind W; mid-high tide on a falling tide; breaks "sometimes".
  https://www.wannasurf.com/spot/Australia_Pacific/Australia/NSW/Sydney_South/gordens_bay_bommie/index.html
- Surf-forecast: exposed reef, inconsistent; ideal swell ENE, offshore W. A review: left is the better wave, short ride
  under 4 ft; about 6 ft it runs over "the Pebble"; a separate outer reef breaks above 3 m.
  https://www.surf-forecast.com/breaks/Clovelly-Bombie
- Note: every dated session found was in a large S-SE event (Aug 2025, Mar 2026), which conflicts with the E-NE
  guidance. Treat the guide direction as unreliable.

**Wedding Cake Island**
- "Rarely breaks, maybe once or twice a year"; works on big south swells from east-coast lows.
  https://tracksmag.com.au/sydney-surfers-feast-on-biggest-waves-ever-seen-at-wedding-cake-island
- Has an outside bank that needs very big swell and an inside ("Southie") section that breaks on less: on 2024-08-01
  and 2025-04-02 "not quite big enough for the outside bank", only the inside working (Surf Check links above).
- Clean conditions in big swell matter: the 28 Mar 2026 session was notable because "not much wind" affected it.
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMUE8tSzCSY

**Malabar**
- Left reef that rolls into a channel; swell S-SE; best wind N, NW, W; starts at 1.0-1.5 m and holds 2 m+; rising tide;
  "fickle ... when it works it really works"; "works best in big swells. When Maroubra is around 6-10 ft, Malabar is
  roughly 3 ft+"; low tide gives a nice wall, mid-high tide when bigger.
  https://www.wannasurf.com/spot/Australia_Pacific/Australia/NSW/Sydney_South/Malabar/index.html
- Surfline: only works on occasion, a rideable alternative on big, windy days.
  https://www.surfline.com/surf-report/malabar/584204204e65fad6a77093f3
- Surf-forecast lists ENE swell, SW wind, all tides, and calls it a "right hand reef"; this contradicts the others,
  so treat it as unreliable. https://www.surf-forecast.com/breaks/Malabar
- Surf Check 2025-04-02: in a huge swell it was "a bit fat ... low tide might be pumping".

**Maroubra North End Point**
- Left-hand point; swell N-NE; wind W-SW; mid tide; starts working at 1.0-1.5 m and holds 3 m+; "rips on a nice big day".
  https://www.wannasurf.com/spot/Australia_Pacific/Australia/NSW/Sydney_South/maroubra_north_end_point/index.html
- Other guides: the north end handles bigger swells and gets hollower; "North end works best on a big swell".
  https://www.surfspots.co/spot/maroubra-north-end-point ; https://thesurfatlas.com/australia-surfing/surfing-in-maroubra/
- Surf Check pattern: in big S swells with southerlies the north end is "a mess" and the south corner is the sheltered
  option (2024-08-02, 2025-05-19, 2025-07-04).

**Bronte Reef / Bunnies**
- Right-hand reef; very consistent (about 150 days a year); works from under 1 m and holds 2 m+; high tide, rising.
  Bunnies (the outer section) only breaks when the swell is bigger, and is hollow or peaky.
  https://www.wannasurf.com/spot/Australia_Pacific/Australia/NSW/Sydney_South/Bronte_Reef/index.html ;
  https://www.surfspots.co/spot/bronte-reef
- Surf-forecast: ideal swell SE, offshore WNW; best season winter; summer mostly flat.
  https://www.surf-forecast.com/breaks/Bronte-Reef
- Surf Check pattern (2023-2026): S/SE swell plus W/NW/SW wind = "hitting the point"; **N swell breaks at the north end
  and leaves the point a mess** (2023-12-19, 2024-03-12/13/20); strong S or NE wind spoils it; it can go "out of
  control" in a mega swell (2025-04-01); "quite fat on the high tide" (2024-11-13), which partly conflicts with the
  WannaSurf high-tide claim.

**Mackenzies**
- Surf-forecast: exposed beach break (sandbar), lefts and rights; ideal swell S; best with WNW offshore; mid tide rising;
  best in winter, July. https://www.surf-forecast.com/breaks/Mckenzies-Bay
- The sand comes and goes; sand builds in calm periods with the right-sized waves and is stripped by serious storms
  (sand present Aug 2023 to at least Feb 2024). https://www.visitbondibeach.com/bondi-blog/2023/8/29/mackenzies-bay-is-back-the-ultimate-sydney-hidden-gem
- Surf Check: best barrels "on this low tide" (2024-05-17); "the swell hitting the wedge nicely" in solid S swell with a
  light S or SW wind (May 2024); a "big blowout" on 2026-03-27 implies the bank had gone. Any model should carry a
  sand-state caveat.

**North Bondi (north corner / Ben Buckler)**
- Ben Buckler: left reef point off the North Bondi headland that "only starts to work on swells over 3 metres".
  https://www.surf-forecast.com/breaks/Bondi-Beach
- North corner: protected from straight E waves by Ben Buckler point, so it gets less energy.
  https://thesurfatlas.com/australia-surfing/bondi-surf/
- Aquabumps (snippet): Bondi "faces South, which is the dominant swell direction"; Bondi "can't handle waves over head
  high"; in a 6 ft S swell North Bondi had "a confused 3 foot hollow left that predominantly shuts down".
  https://www.aquabumps.com/tag/big-swell/
- Surf Check: when Bronte/Tama were unsurfable after a big overnight jump with offshore wind, "Ben Buckler and North
  Bondi is a lot cleaner" (2024-07-14); in a huge swell, surfers "crammed into the north corner" (2024-03-05); with
  messy south-end conditions, the "North end is much cleaner" (2023-07-23). This supports treating it as the sheltered
  option.
