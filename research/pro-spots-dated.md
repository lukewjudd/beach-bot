# Pros-only and big-wave spots: locations, conditions, viewing points and dated sessions

Compiled 10 Nov 2026 for a "Pros only" tier: scored and shown, never in the surf call or calendar, so people know
when to go and watch. This builds on `eastern-reefs-dated.md`, `northern-reefs-dated.md` and `surf-history.md`.
Rows already in those files are marked *(prior)* and only summarised. Everything else is new.

Verdicts: **GOOD** (ridden and working), **OK** (ridden, raw or mixed), **POOR** (swell present but the spot not
working: too windy, wrong direction, too small or too big for it), **FLAT** (not breaking), **breaking** (breaking,
but the source doesn't say how it surfed). Dates are AEST/AEDT. *inferred* means the date was worked out from the
publish date plus "yesterday", "last Thursday" and the like. For YouTube, the upload time was converted to Sydney
time, and a date in a title is used only when the upload time is consistent with it.

## Sources and how they were used

- **YouTube.** The public search and watch pages were read with a plain request, with no bot check. That gives titles,
  upload times and descriptions. Useful channels: We Bodyboard and skymonkey (drone, dated titles), SURFING VISIONS
  (Tim Bonython), Nathan Florence, Thrills Spillz, **Sydney Surf Daily** (dated "Sydney Surf Report <spot>" clips;
  the title names the spot but the description gives no verdict), Big Wave Challenge entries (exact dates).
- **Sprout Daily** (sproutdaily.com/the-daily/D-mon-YY). A Manly photo blog. Every day in 22 big-swell windows from
  2022 to 2026 was checked, which gave 47 posts. It covers the Queenscliff Bombie and Deadmans.
- **Tracks** (fetchable), Carve, Stab, Yardbarker (republishes Surfer), Surfbugle, Frothers.
- **Spot guides:** Wannasurf (Sydney South and Sydney North pages, plus each spot page), surf-forecast.com,
  worldbeachguide. **OSM Nominatim** gave the coordinates of viewing points.
- **Blocked, so search snippets only:** swellnet.com, theinertia.com, coogeenews.com.au. mondo.surf and the NPWS
  lookout URL returned 404. Wayback was not used.

## 1. Cape Solander ("Ours", "Cape Fear", "Pikers Hole"), Kurnell

**What and where.** A heavy right-hand slab over a rock shelf in front of a cliff, in Kamay Botany Bay National Park
on the ocean (east) side of the Kurnell peninsula. There is also a rarer left
([We Bodyboard, 1 Jun 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lf_cF1H1x_Y); [Tracks](https://tracksmag.com.au/watch-the-rare-cape-solander-left-561589)).
- Wannasurf pin: **34°0.631'S 151°13.844'E (-34.0105, 151.2307)**, marked "approximate"; walk-in under 5 minutes.
  [Wannasurf](https://www.wannasurf.com/spot/Australia_Pacific/Australia/NSW/Sydney_South/ours/index.html)
- OSM puts the cape at **-34.0166, 151.2318** and the whale-watching platform at -34.0171, 151.2309. The Wannasurf pin
  is about 700 m north of these, and probably sits on the cliff rather than in the water. No source gives a pin that is
  clearly in the water. **Suggested pin:** about 100 m seaward of the cape point (roughly -34.0165, 151.2330). This is
  derived, so check it against aerial photos of the shelf before using it.
- It breaks "just meters away from the cliff" ([Tracks, 30 Apr 2026](https://tracksmag.com.au/kipp-caddy-scores-cape-solander-and-shark-island-in-episode-one-of-slab-sessions)).
  Surfers exit by jumping onto the cliff to the right of the break (Wannasurf).

**Level.** "Pros or very experienced surfers only". Wannasurf also tells spectators to watch, not paddle out.

**Conditions by source.**

Disagreement: Wannasurf says SE/E, while Bonython says E/NE. The sessions back E as the main direction, with SE also
working. No source gives a swell period.

**Where to watch from.** The **Cape Solander whale-watching platform**, which was rebuilt in Dec 2022 and holds about
60 people. It is next to the car park and accessible parking on Cape Solander Drive. Gates are open 7am–7:30pm, the
Kurnell area charges **$8 per vehicle**, and the cliffs are unfenced
([NPWS](https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/things-to-do/Lookouts/Cape-Solander)). The surfed reefs are "not far
from the whale watching platform" ([Illawarra Mercury, 2018](https://www.illawarramercury.com.au/story/5541426/incredible-photo-captures-surfers-close-encounter-with-whales-at-kurnell/)).
Filmers and crowds stand on the cliff top above the slab. One filmer said "it's on the cliff of Cape Solander" that
he wants to be watching ([YT, 14 Apr 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p7kakprioQA)). Distance: tens of metres
from the cliff edge, and about 100–700 m from the platform depending on which pin is right. **Safety:** in June 2024
two people drowned after being swept off the rocks at nearby Yena Gap ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Solander)).
Any "go and watch" copy should say to stay back from the edge.

## 2. Voodoo, Kurnell (south side). It is NOT near Malabar or Little Bay

**What and where.** A heavy reef on the **south-facing side of the Kurnell peninsula, inside Bate Bay**, opposite
Cronulla. Surfbugle calls it "the most northerly ledge among a huge number of south-facing reefs", at the northern
end of Cronulla Beach, and less than five miles from the airport runway
([Surfbugle, 4 Jun 2024](https://surfbugle.substack.com/p/spot-check-voodoo)).
- Wannasurf pin: **34°2.612'S 151°12.51'E (-34.0435, 151.2085)**, "about 500m right of Suck Rock". Suck Rock's pin is
  34°2.607'S 151°12.758'E. [Wannasurf Voodoo](https://www.wannasurf.com/spot/Australia_Pacific/Australia/NSW/Sydney_South/voodoo/index.html)
- Surf-forecast: "A left hand reef ... located 500 metres South of Potters Point", 34.04°S 151.21°E. Its review warns
  you can be washed toward "the rocks at Boat Harbour or the Mays Reefs". [surf-forecast](https://www.surf-forecast.com/breaks/Voodoo)
- OSM puts Potter Point at -34.0418, 151.2150, so the Wannasurf pin is about 650 m WSW of it. The reef is 300–500 m
  out (Wannasurf says "paddle 300-500m"). **Facing about 180–200°**, unlike every other spot in the bot.
- Mainly a left: holds about 10 ft and starts about 4 ft. There is also a right that closes out above about 6 ft
  (Surfbugle).

**Region note.** This is Sutherland Shire and Bate Bay, so it belongs with Cronulla's swell window, not the eastern
suburbs. If it's added, it needs its own facing, because S swell goes straight in.

**Conditions by source.**

| Source | Swell from | Size | Wind | Tide |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wannasurf | SW, S, SE | starts at 1.5–2 m (5–6 ft), holds 5 m+ (16 ft+); "holds 20ft in a right south swell" | NW, W, SW | all |
| Surfbugle | S, "from deep water in the bay" | left starts about 4 ft and holds about 10 ft; ideal 6–8 ft | light NW best; "handles the north-east summer breeze" | left on low tide (a big low tide is ideal); right only at high |
| surf-forecast | S | – | NNE | all; "best around half tide or low tide when small"; higher tides make it less hollow |

Agreement: S swell, a N to NW wind, and lower tides for the left. Wannasurf says it is most consistent from December
to March (summer NE winds are offshore there).

**Where to watch from.** No source names a viewing point. The nearest public headlands are **Potter Point and Cape
Baily** in Kamay Botany Bay NP, on the Cape Baily coast track. Access is via the Kurnell dirt or 4x4 tracks past the
desalination plant (Wannasurf; Surfbugle). Distance is about 0.5–0.7 km. **Low confidence**, and the access road may
be private or gated in places.

## 3. Honeycomb ("honeycombe"). It is at Lurline Bay, not Wylies or Coogee

- Wannasurf: **33°56.328'S 151°15.818'E (-33.9388, 151.2636)**, alternative name "lurline bay". "Drive to the end of
  the street and get down the rocks". It is next to "the small rockpool", with Lurline across the bay off the southern
  headland. A right reef, "Pros or kamikaze only", "sometimes breaks". Swell SE/E/NE, starts at 1.0–1.5 m and holds 4 m+;
  wind W; mid tide, falling. A contributor note says low tide is best, and that Lurline is more consistent.
  [Wannasurf](https://www.wannasurf.com/spot/Australia_Pacific/Australia/NSW/Sydney_South/honeycomb/index.html)
- worldbeachguide puts it 0.5 mi from Maroubra North End Point. OSM: Lurline Bay is at -33.9375, 151.2618 and Mahon
  Pool at -33.9430, 151.2639, so "the small rockpool" is probably Mahon Pool.
- **It is about 100 m from the existing `Lurline Bay` pin (-33.9378, 151.2638).** No dated session names Honeycomb
  (one 2021 clip "Surfing. Honeycomb. Eastern Beaches" has no date: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mV5YA0G9bx4).
- Watch from: the Marine Parade clifftops and Mahon Pool (Jack Vanny Reserve). Surf Check filmed Lurline from there on
  1 Apr 2025 ("Mahon pool is just 200 meters from L[urline]") https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kzsnkzz0GL4
- Related Lurline days (for the existing entry, not Honeycomb): **2025-04-01** GOOD, "massive pumping right handers
  peeling off the point all day", two chargers paddled out at 4:20pm (Surf Check, above). **2022-04-02** GOOD (XL),
  "big enough for a tow in at Lurline Bay" (uploaded 2 Apr 2022, 13:52 AEST) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OtWJ6pAg-os

**Recommendation:** don't add it as its own spot. Fold it into `Lurline Bay` and mention it in that card's text.

## 4. Ben Buckler and Wedding Cake Island (already `level="expert"`): viewing points

Both are already in `SURF_SPOTS` as expert-level (`min_hs` 2.0 and 2.5, `at_pin`, `out_to_sea`). Confirmed. The only
new material is where to watch from.

- **Ben Buckler** (pin -33.8942, 151.2868; OSM Ben Buckler -33.8915, 151.2842). Watch from the **Ben Buckler headland
  at the north end of Bondi** (Ray O'Keefe Reserve and the clifftop at the end of Ramsgate Ave), about 200–400 m away.
  On 1 Apr 2025 the riders were "spurred on by the crowd of onlookers gathered on the headland above", and Hardaker
  said "people on the cliffs were whistling" ([Tracks](https://tracksmag.com.au/hunting-sydneys-mega-swell-with-chase-hardaker)).
- **Wedding Cake Island** (OSM islet -33.9259, 151.2654; pin -33.9264, 151.2672). Watch from **Coogee's southern
  headland: Grant Reserve (-33.9247, 151.2581, about 700 m) and Trenerry Reserve (-33.9279, 151.2602, about 550 m)**,
  or Dunningham Reserve on the north headland, which is further. Coogee News (snippet; blocked) says onlookers
  "gathered at elevated vantage points, including nearby reserves" ([coogeenews](https://coogeenews.com.au/?p=13483)).
  The outside bank breaks about 100 m beyond the island, and an outer reef "a km out to sea" ([Tracks 2023](https://tracksmag.com.au/sydney-surfers-feast-on-biggest-waves-ever-seen-at-wedding-cake-island)).
  Binoculars help.

## 5. Queenscliff Bombie ("Queensie Bombie", "Queenscliff Bombora"), Manly

**Where.** Wannasurf pin **33°47.352'S 151°17.692'E (-33.7892, 151.2949)**, a deep-water boulder bombora off Queenscliff
headland that breaks left and right (*prior*). It is about 600 m ESE of Queenscliff Rock Pool (OSM -33.7863, 151.2895).

**Conditions.** Wannasurf: starts at 2.5–3 m and holds 5 m+, all tides, "pros or kamikaze only". Tracks: needs "the
perfect combination of East swell and offshore winds"; before 2025 it last broke properly in 2016 (*prior*). New
evidence:
- **E swell is what turns it on.** It worked on 26 Mar 2026 ("hefty east swell") and in Apr and Aug 2025 (both E).
- **Big S swells only "tickle" it.** On 2024-06-17 (a big S swell) "raw southern energy wrapping in ... tickling the
  depths of the Queenscliff Bombora", but "proper waves were few and far between". On 2022-04-01 it was "just starting
  to stir" in a building S swell. On 2022-04-02 (6 m+ S) it broke with no takers (*prior*).
- Tide: on 2026-04-13 Sprout said it was "slow going", and "with the tide about to hit low, things might liven up". That
  suggests lower tide is better, which disagrees mildly with Wannasurf's "all tides".

**Where to watch from.** **Queenscliff headland** (the rock pool and the clifftop by Greycliffe St; Sprout shoots from
"my vantage at Queenscliff at dawn"), about 600 m. Also the Freshwater headland and Harbord Diggers clifftop
(-33.7791, 151.2930), about 1.1 km. On Easter 2025 "numerous filmers and [an] extensive crowd ... lined the headland"
([Tracks](https://tracksmag.com.au/tom-myers-on-the-wave-of-his-life-at-sydneys-queenscliff-bombie), search snippet).

## 6. Long Reef Bombie, Collaroy

**Where.** Wannasurf pin **33°44.754'S 151°18.502'E (-33.7459, 151.3084)** (*prior*). It sits about 0.5–0.9 km SW of
Long Reef Point (OSM -33.7420, 151.3171), on the Dee Why side of the headland.

**Conditions** (*prior*). Wannasurf: S/SE/E swell, 1–1.5 m up to 5 m+, N/NW/W/NE wind, low to mid tide, pros only.
surf-forecast: SE swell, NW wind, mid tide rising, "rarely breaks". New evidence: **every dated 2022+ session was a
big S swell** (June 2022, Feb 2024). Long Reef's other reefs ("Germans & Frenchmans") were "massive but battered by
winds" in the 8 m S storm swell of 2023-05-08, so wind matters here.

**Disagreement worth noting.** One 2024 clip suggests it is ridden more often, and on smaller swells, than "pros only"
implies: a "3m Southerly Swell" with low wind gave "a few epic long rides". Some 2021 and 2026 clips are surfski or
drone footage of the bombora. "Pros only" may be too strict for the inner bombie, while the outer bombie is a
big-wave spot.

**Where to watch from.** The **Long Reef headland**: the public walking track around the golf course and the point,
in the Long Reef Aquatic Reserve. The golf club is at -33.7387, 151.3067. Distance is about 0.7–0.9 km.

## 7. Deadmans ("Deadies"), under the Shelly / North Head cliffs, Manly

**Where.** Wannasurf pin **33°48.018'S 151°18.028'E (-33.8003, 151.3005)**, a right slab off the ocean side of the
Shelly headland (*prior*). WSL: "only 30 metres from the steep cliffs" and "often bottoms out on the shallow ledge"
([WSL, 16 Jul 2020](https://www.worldsurfleague.com/posts/453748/sydneys-northern-beaches-pump-on-one-of-the-heaviest-swells-in-years)).

**Conditions** (*prior*). Wannasurf: S/SE/E swell, starts at 2.5–3 m and holds 5 m+, wind W/SW/S/SE/E, mid to high
tide, "about 5 days a year". New evidence: it works on **straight S swells** (188°, 2022-04-02; 8 m S on 2023-05-08;
S on 2024-06-18; "Deady's South Swell 28th-29th of March" 2026) and on **E swells** (2025-08-05). The bigger 2024 swell
was "high period", with "unusual ... grunt and push" (Dodshon, undated). Over-size kills it: on 2025-07-01 it went
"from 1ft early to 6ft and pumping by 9 o'clock to 8-10ft by lunchtime", and "looked amazing before it got too big".

**Where to watch from.** The **Shelly headland car park and cliffs** at the end of Bluefish Dr, above the break, about
150–300 m away (OSM Shelly Headland -33.7994, 151.2974). Sprout: "great pictures from the Bower cliffs", "raw southern
energy wrapping in under the cliffs of The Bower Carpark", and the caption "Subtle message from the carpark" (31 Jul
2024). Swellnet calls it an "up close and personal vantage point" (snippet). Of all the spots here, this is the best
one for spectators.

**Correction to `northern-reefs-dated.md`:** its Deadmans row "2022-01-02 surfed (Swell Diary)" is a misread. The
Swell Diary entry's own title is "DEADMANS **02.04.2022** RAW FOOTAGE", which is 2 April 2022 (d.m.y). Swell Diary
also lists a "Thu 2 Apr 2020" entry titled "Sunday April 2nd", which is probably 2022 as well. **Drop 2022-01-02.**

## 8. Other pro-rated spots in the two regions (checked, mostly excluded)

From Wannasurf's "Pros or kamikaze only" ratings on the [Sydney South](https://www.wannasurf.com/spot/Australia_Pacific/Australia/NSW/Sydney_South/index.html)
and [Sydney North](https://www.wannasurf.com/spot/Australia_Pacific/Australia/NSW/Sydney_North/index.html) lists:

- **Diamonds (Diamond Bay, Vaucluse/Dover Heights):** 33°51.75'S 151°16.994'E (-33.8625, 151.2832). A right reef,
  S/SE/E swell, 1.0–1.5 m up to 3 m+, mid-high rising, pros only. Access is a ladder off the cliff walk. Watch from
  Diamond Bay Reserve (-33.8620, 151.2819), about 100 m away. **North of Bondi, just outside the stated region.** No
  dated sessions found. [Wannasurf](https://www.wannasurf.com/spot/Australia_Pacific/Australia/NSW/Sydney_South/Diamonds/index.html)
- **Diggers Rights (Freshwater):** 33°46.786'S 151°17.783'E (-33.7798, 151.2964), below the Harbord Diggers. A right
  slab, E swell, starts at **over 3.5 m** and holds 5 m+, NW/W/SW wind, "rarely breaks, about 5 days a year", "never
  surf this spot alone". You watch from the clifftop over the ledge. No dated sessions found.
  [Wannasurf](https://www.wannasurf.com/spot/Australia_Pacific/Australia/NSW/Sydney_North/Diggers_Rights/index.html)
- **Kelso Beach:** pros only, a right slab, S/SE/E, 1.5–2 m up to 5 m+, but "GPS coordinates not set" and no location
  given. Unknown.
- **Castle corner, Curl Curl:** Wannasurf's "pros" rating is wrong; it's a beach-break left, and a commenter says so.
- **Suck Rock (Kurnell):** next to Voodoo (-34.0435, 151.2126). Rated "experienced", not pros; a right ledge, S/SE/E,
  1–2.5 m, N/NW/W wind, mid tide.
- **Newport Crosswaves and Plungers:** Wannasurf rates both "Experienced", not pros. Crosswaves is a "world class"
  right ledge, "regular"; Plungers works at high tide only and "rarely" breaks, says a local. No dated sessions.
- **Lighthouse Rights (Barrenjoey):** Wannasurf rates it "Experienced", with no GPS, and its own data is garbled
  (a "West" swell and "East" wind). It is reached by paddling round the point. A 2006 local comment says big swells
  break "off the tip of the point". No dated sessions.
- **Unnamed bombies seen on 2023-05-08:** "a mysto bombie off Curl Curl" about 2 km out ("never seen this bombie
  break before") and "Northy Bombie, 4 x Overhead" (North Narrabeen) ([Sprout](https://www.sproutdaily.com/the-daily/8-may-23)).
  Neither has a public guide entry.
- **Shark Island (Cronulla):** Sydney's best-known slab, but it's in Cronulla, outside both regions. Mentioned only so
  its absence is deliberate.

### Exclude (for now), and why

- **Honeycomb:** it's the reef next to the existing Lurline Bay pin (about 100 m away), has no dated sessions, and the
  "Wylies/Coogee" location in the brief is wrong. Fold it into Lurline's text.
- **Voodoo:** add it only if you're happy for the bot to cover the Kurnell/Bate Bay south side. It has 1 dated session,
  no source names a viewing point, and access is by 4x4 track. If it is added, give it a ~190° facing and its own swell
  window. Otherwise exclude it.
- **Diggers Rights, Diamonds:** real, publicly named and pros-only, but no dated sessions, and Diamonds is north of
  Bondi.
- **Kelso Beach:** location unknown.
- **Newport Crosswaves and Plungers:** rated "experienced", not pros, and no dated evidence (unchanged from the
  northern file).
- **Lighthouse Rights:** no pin, garbled guide data, no dated sessions; "rarely surfed / tow-in" (prior).
- **Curl Curl and North Narrabeen bombies:** seen once, with no public name or guide.
- **Shark Island:** outside both regions.

### Notes for the backtest

1. The negatives are thin everywhere. The only explicit POOR/FLAT rows are for the Queenscliff Bombie (5) and one
   too-big afternoon at Deadmans. For Solander and Long Reef, "no report" on a big-swell day should count as unknown,
   not poor.
2. The **Queenscliff Bombie direction rule is the clearest signal**: E swell works, and a big S swell (2022-04-01/02,
   2024-06-17) only "stirs" or "tickles" it. Use `works_from` of about (60, 130).
3. The **Deadmans S-swell days** (2022-04-02, 2023-05-08, 2024-06-18, 2026-03-28/29) match the Wedding Cake and
   Ben Buckler XL S days, so all three should light up together on big S events.
4. **2026-03-26** (E swell) is a good test day: Solander, the Queenscliff Bombie and Clovelly Bombie were all ridden,
   while Wedding Cake wasn't reported until the S swell on the 28th.
