# Eastern beaches surf history (Jan 2023 to Nov 2026)

Dated days where a source says how the surf actually was at Bondi (incl. south Bondi / Ben Buckler), Tamarama, Bronte, Clovelly, Coogee, Maroubra and Malabar/Little Bay. Use these as ground truth for backtesting. Compiled 9 Nov 2026.

## Sources, and how far to trust them

- **Most useful:** Tracks Magazine (tracksmag.com.au) swell recaps. They are dated, name the beach and often the wind. Also Weatherzone and ABC for buoy numbers on big events. Surfer.com stories (read via their Yahoo syndication) covered the March 2026 swell.
- **The "Surf Check" YouTube channel** posts a dated surf check most mornings for Bronte/Tamarama, Bondi and South Maroubra, with a short description of conditions. These are a single person's description, so they are rated medium or low confidence. They are still the best source of exact dates for ordinary good and poor days.
- **Aquabumps** (Bondi daily photo blog) and **Rand Leeb-du Toit's Substack** both give dated Bondi descriptions.
- **Blocked sites (HTTP 403):** Swellnet, Aquabumps, Randwick and Waverley council pages, and The Inertia all blocked WebFetch, and coastalwatch.com now redirects to Surfline. For these the facts come from search-engine snippets only, which is weaker.
- **Swellnet forecaster notes are forecasts, not observations.** They are cited only to back up an observed day. Two forecast-only days were left out: 5 Aug 2025 (called "potentially epic" under 8ft E swell with offshore winds) and 29 Mar 2026 (called "more manageable, 4-6ft easing").
- **Missing:** no Surfing NSW or WSL contest results at Maroubra or Bondi turned up for 2023-2026.
- **Dates:** where a source says only "Tuesday" or "last weekend", the date is worked out from the publish date. Those rows say so.

Categories: **good** = good surf reported; **big-bad** = large but blown out, closed, closing out or dangerous; **flat** = flat or very poor.
