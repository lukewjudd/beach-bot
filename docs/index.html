How it works

Spots

    Conditions

    Each morning (before 9am on weekdays, before 11am on weekends) and evening (after 5pm on weekdays, after 2pm on weekends) is scored out of 10. The score uses wind, swell, air and water temperature, rain, water quality and local knowledge of each beach, judged on that spot's best two hours. Sessions that score 8/10 or better get posted to the calendar, and 9+ gets a 🔥. Scores refresh at about 4am, 3pm and 7pm.
